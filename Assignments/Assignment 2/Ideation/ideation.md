# Wakili - Legal Platform Ideation

## Problem Statement

Many people in Egypt struggle to find trusted, verified lawyers quickly — especially in urgent legal situations. The process is opaque, expensive, and inaccessible to the average person.

## Proposed Solutions

- **Lawyer Directory:** A searchable directory of verified lawyers filtered by specialization, price, location, and availability.
- **Contract Analysis:** AI-powered contract analysis tool to help users understand legal documents without needing a lawyer.
- **Emergency Chatbot:** An emergency Arabic-language legal chatbot powered by the Egyptian Constitution.
- **Booking System:** A streamlined appointment booking system with cash-on-session payment for accessibility.
